Visit the Wellness Center

Want to visit? Here is some important information to know before coming.

When Can You Visit?

During Class

Students need a pass from their teacher and can stay for one class period.

During Lunch

Students can also visit the Wellness Center during lunch.

Passing Periods

Students can visit during passing periods.

Where Are We Located?

The Wellness Center is located in the main building downstairs, next to the cafeteria.

Oakland Technical High School

4351 Broadway, Oakland, CA 94611

Need Support?

Students can use the self-referral form to request support from the Wellness Center.
