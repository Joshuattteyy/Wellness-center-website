Programs and Services

The Wellness Center offers different types of support to help students succeed both in and out of school.

Individual Counseling

Students can receive individual support with personal and emotional challenges.

Stress Management

Learn coping strategies and ways to deal with everyday stress.

Conflict Resolution

Get help working through disagreements.

Wellness Groups

Participate in activities and groups that encourage student well-being.

Wellness Ambassadors

Learn about peer-to-peer wellness support and student leadership.

Family and Community Support

Connect with family support services and helpful community resources.
